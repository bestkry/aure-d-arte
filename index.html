§ I

The four plates

Four moving images. Each looped without cut, each begun where it will end. They are the volume's argument: that stillness is a place the eye consents to visit, never a state the picture is ever truly in.

§ II

The catalogue

Fifteen impressions organised by index rather than category. Each work is signed, numbered, and dispatched in an archival sleeve within seven working days.

§ III A note on provenance

Aure d'Arte is a small editorial house that publishes cinematic plates and pigment prints in editions of two hundred and fifty. Each work begins as a machine-guided study and ends as a hand-finished object — printed on 310-gsm cotton rag with archival pigment inks, signed in graphite, and stamped with the studio's blind seal.

We do not warehouse. We do not restock. When an edition is closed, it is closed for good; the plate is retired and the file archived offline. What you acquire is not a reproduction of a moving image — it is the last translation of one, and the only one that will exist at that size, on that paper, in that hand.

The volume is issued once. The next is called Ossuary and will appear when it is ready.

§ IV

Write to acquire.

Purchases are placed by correspondence. State the plate and impression size, and we will send a hand-composed reply — usually the same day, always within two. Studio hours are quiet; replies are considered.
